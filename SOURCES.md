## Tasarım ilkesi
- Yayınlanmış veya kılavuzlarda kullanılan şiddet bantları varsa sonuç kartında gösterilir.
- Literatürde birden fazla eşik sistemi varsa kaynak ve belirsizlik açıkça belirtilir (özellikle PDAI).
- Evrensel doğrulanmış şiddet eşiği bulunmayan NAPSI gibi skorlar için yapay sınıflama üretilmez.
- SCORTEN sonucu hastalık şiddeti etiketi değil, orijinal kohorta dayalı prognostik mortalite yüzdesi olarak sunulur.

## LPPAI
- Chiang C, Sah D, Cho BK, Ochoa BE, Price VH. Hydroxychloroquine and lichen planopilaris: efficacy and introduction of Lichen Planopilaris Activity Index scoring system. J Am Acad Dermatol. 2010;62:387–392. PMID: 20061052. DOI: 10.1016/j.jaad.2009.08.054.
- LPPAI formula: (pruritus + pain + burning)/3 + (scalp erythema + perifollicular erythema + perifollicular scale)/3 + 2.5×pull test + 1.5×(spreading/2); range 0–10.

## mLoSSI
- Arkachaisri T, et al. The localized scleroderma skin severity index and physician global assessment of disease activity: a work in progress toward development of localized scleroderma outcome measures. J Rheumatol. 2009;36:2819–2829. PMID: 19833758.
- Kelsey CE, Torok KS. The Localized Scleroderma Cutaneous Assessment Tool: responsiveness to change in a pediatric clinical population. J Am Acad Dermatol. 2013;69:214–220. PMID: 23562760. DOI: 10.1016/j.jaad.2013.02.007.
- Standalone mLoSSI evaluates erythema (0–3), skin thickness (0–3), and a new lesion/extension within the past month (0 or 3) across 18 anatomic sites; maximum 162.
- mLoSSI is retained as the earlier activity instrument. The current LoSCAT activity index (LoSAI) uses **induration at the lesion edge instead of skin thickness**; therefore the LoSAI severity bands below are not automatically applied to the standalone mLoSSI calculator.

## LoSCAT / LoSAI / LoSDI
- Teske NM, Jacobe HT. Using the Localized Scleroderma Cutaneous Assessment Tool (LoSCAT) to classify morphoea by severity and identify clinically significant change. Br J Dermatol. 2020;182:398–404. PMID: 31049928.
- Current LoSCAT separately quantifies **activity (LoSAI)** and **damage (LoSDI)** and also includes Physician Global Assessment of activity (PGA-A) and damage (PGA-D).
- **LoSAI:** 18 sites × [new/enlarged in past month (0 or 3) + erythema (0–3) + induration at the lesion edge (0–3)]; range 0–162.
- **LoSDI:** 18 sites × [dermal atrophy (0–3) + subcutaneous/deep atrophy (0–3) + dyspigmentation (0–3) + central sclerosis/skin thickness (0–3)]; range 0–216.
- **PGA-A / PGA-D:** 0–100 visual/global physician assessments; higher scores indicate more severe activity or damage.
- Teske & Jacobe severity bands: LoSAI 0–4 mild, 5–12 moderate, ≥13 severe; LoSDI 0–10 mild, 11–15 moderate, ≥16 severe; PGA-A 0–10 / 11–30 / ≥31; PGA-D 0–18 / 19–30 / ≥31. These are study-derived severity bands, not universal treatment thresholds.
- Earlier LoSCAT versions used mLoSSI and a three-domain LoSDI. The v1.6.7 LoSCAT page follows the current form described by Teske & Jacobe, while mLoSSI remains available as a separate historical activity calculator.
- Arkachaisri T, et al. Development and initial validation of the Localized Scleroderma Skin Damage Index and Physician Global Assessment of disease Damage. Rheumatology (Oxford). 2010;49:373–381. PMID: 20008472.
- Skrzypek-Salamon A, et al. Localized Scleroderma Cutaneous Assessment Tool adapted for use in adult patients. Health Qual Life Outcomes. 2018;16:185. PMID: 30217204. DOI: 10.1186/s12955-018-1010-z.
